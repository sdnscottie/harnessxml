# Code of Conduct

## The standard

Argue about the specification as hard as you like. Never about the person.

Technical disagreement is the mechanism by which a specification gets good.
A proposal being wrong is a normal, expected, useful outcome — saying so
directly is not rudeness, and taking it personally is not a violation. What is
out of bounds is anything aimed at the human rather than the argument.

## Expected

- Critique the design, the wording, the tradeoff, the test coverage.
- Say "this is wrong, and here is the case it breaks on."
- Assume the other person read the spec and is arguing in good faith.
- Accept that the editors will sometimes decide against you, in writing, and
  that the written reason is the point.

## Not accepted

- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments about a person or group.
- Harassment of any kind, publicly or privately.
- Discriminatory language or conduct regarding gender, gender identity or
  expression, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size,
  race, ethnicity, age, religion, or nationality.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, or relitigating a decided HXEP without
  new information.

## Scope

Applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, HXEP discussions, and any space
where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report to **conduct@harnessxml.com**. Reports are handled confidentially by
the steward (VisML). Response is proportionate: usually a private word, and
where necessary editing or removing content, or a temporary or permanent ban
from project spaces.

If the report concerns the steward, and you would rather not raise it with
them, say so in the report — an uninvolved third party will be asked to
handle it.

## Attribution
